# Master Kingdom: Privacy Policy (draft)

_Last updated: [DATE]. Replace the bracketed items before publishing, and have it reviewed for the countries you publish in. This draft describes what the game does today; it makes no legal-compliance claims._

**What the game stores.** Game progress and settings (chosen character colours, learning level, completed puzzles, stars, crystals, treasures, volume settings) and an optional nickname if a player types one. This is saved only in the browser or app storage on the device (localStorage). It is never sent to us or anyone else.

**What the game does not do.** No accounts, emails, passwords, chat, public profiles, advertising, analytics, tracking, location, camera, microphone or in-app purchases. The game loads no third-party scripts, fonts or services once installed.

**Narration.** Spoken narration uses the text-to-speech voices built into the device. Some devices may process speech through their own voice service, depending on the device's settings. The game itself sends nothing.

**Parent Area.** Protected by a simple maths question so young children do not open it by accident. It is not a security feature. It shows progress on the device and can reset all progress.

**Deleting data.** Parent Area > Reset Progress, or clear the site/app data in the browser or Android settings. Progress does not move between devices.
